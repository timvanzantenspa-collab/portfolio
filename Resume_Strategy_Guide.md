## HOW TO CHOOSE YOUR VERSION

### Rule of thumb:

**1. Read the job description carefully**

Look for keywords and emphasis:

- **Lots of keywords about:** teaching, workshops, youth, education, media literacy, digital skills?  
  → **Use Version 2 (Education Focus)**

- **Lots of keywords about:** research, insights, strategy, analysis, programming, audience, data?  
  → **Use Version 3 (Research & Strategy)**

- **Mixed or unclear emphasis?** Or emphasizes: communication, content, coordination, production?  
  → **Use Version 1 (Universal)**

### 2. Organization type:

- **Education, libraries, youth organizations, NGOs focused on media**  
  → Version 2

- **Cultural institutions (museums, festivals, venues), research institutes, policy organizations**  
  → Version 3

- **Communications departments, generalist cultural roles, event coordination**  
  → Version 1

### 3. When in doubt:
- Always use Version 1 (Universal)
- It has the highest overall ATS keyword density
- It's your most flexible position

## KEY STRATEGIC IMPROVEMENTS ACROSS ALL VERSIONS

### 1. Professional Profile
**Old tone:** "Hi, I'm a creative person with interesting interests"

**New tone:** "Multidisciplinary professional with expertise in [specific area]"

This immediately signals:
- Structured thinking
- Clear professional identity
- Ability to articulate value
- Strategic self-awareness

### 2. Skills Section
This is what ATS systems scan first (after keywords in profile).

**Added explicitly:**
- Communication & content
- Project & production
- Research & digital
- Tools & platforms
- Languages

**Why this matters:** You have these skills but ATS wouldn't find them without this explicit list.

### 3. Stronger Verbs & Impact
**Old:** "Analyseerde 15 jaar ticketingdata en genereerde zo publieksinzichten..."

**New:** "Analyzed 15 years of ticketing data (10,000+ records) to generate strategic audience insights for programming decisions"

**Why this works:**
- Stronger action verbs (Analyzed, Generated, Created, Supported)
- Quantification (10,000+ records, 300+ students, 15 years)
- Clear business impact
- Professional tone
- More ATS keywords

### 4. Measurable Outcomes
**Added:** Student numbers, time periods, scale of events, data volume

This increases credibility enormously.

### 5. URL & Contact
**Added:** Your website URL prominently

This is critical because:
- ATS systems can find you
- Recruiters can see your actual work
- Your site reinforces your brand
- It shows you're professional and findable

### 6. One-column, clean layout
- All versions use standard formatting
- No columns, graphics, or icons
- ATS-friendly
- Professional and minimal
- Aligned with your invoice/brand aesthetic

## POSITION vs. IDENTITY

**Important distinction:**

Your **identity** is genuinely multidisciplinary.

Your **position** is where you lead with different strengths depending on context.

This is not dishonest. It's strategic communication.

Example:
- A musician who also teaches piano doesn't lead with "teacher" when applying to be a performer
- A researcher who also writes doesn't lead with "writer" when applying for analyst roles
- You're the same person. You're just emphasizing what's most relevant.
